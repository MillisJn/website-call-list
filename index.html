How to reach them

  1. Call first. Trades pick up 7–9am or 4–6pm, before and after job sites. Salons and barbers answer best mid-morning on weekdays.
  2. If an email is listed, send a short note the same day with a one-page mock-up built from their Facebook photos or logo.
  3. If they have a Facebook page, message it too. Owners without a website usually run the business from Facebook.
  4. Pitch: homeowners in Indian Hill, Hyde Park and Montgomery search Google before they call. Right now they find a directory page or nothing. Show their listing next to a competitor's site.
  5. Offer a flat-price starter site plus Google Business Profile setup. Contractors want leads and to look established. Salons want online booking.

Source: YellowPages listings without a website, checked by web search in November 2026. Phone numbers and emails are as publicly listed. Call notes are stored in the owner's Google Sheet. Confirm before you put anyone on a mailing list.
